Questions people ask first

Will I have to give up roti, rice or sweets?

No. We work on portions, timing and balance with the food you already eat. Nothing is banned.

I'm vegetarian. Does this still work?

Yes. Plans are built around your own preferences, whether vegetarian, eggetarian or non-vegetarian.

Is this medical advice?

No. I'm a nutrition coach, not a doctor or clinical dietitian. If you have a medical condition, I'll ask you to keep your doctor in the loop and we'll work alongside their guidance.

Do we meet in person?

Coaching is online, over video calls and WhatsApp, so you can join from anywhere.

I travel a lot. Is this coaching for me?

Absolutely. Everything happens online, so your coaching travels with you. We'll plan for hotel breakfasts, airport food, client dinners and eating out, so you know what to pick wherever you are, and you can check in with me on WhatsApp from any city.
